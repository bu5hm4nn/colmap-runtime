# Third-party components and distribution conditions

This image packages PyCOLMAP's structure-from-motion and multi-view stereo
application functionality and its runtime dependencies. It does not relicense
third-party software. It is not a general CUDA SDK redistribution.

## NVIDIA components

The base is NVIDIA's CUDA 12.9.1 runtime image. Preserve its included notices and
licences. The pinned Python packages nvidia-cuda-runtime-cu12 12.9.79 and
nvidia-curand-cu12 10.3.10.19 retain their original License.txt files in their
installed .dist-info directories. libcudart.so and libcurand.so are listed in
CUDA EULA Attachment A; their distribution remains conditional on the EULA.

CUDA EULA: https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/archive/12.9.1/eula/index.html

Section 1.1.2 requires material additional application functionality, application-
only access to redistributed SDK portions, consistent distribution terms and
other conditions. NVIDIA components here are provided for the included PyCOLMAP
application, not independently licensed for arbitrary redistribution. No new
licence grant for NVIDIA components is made by this repository.

cuda-toolkit 12.9.2.0 is a metadata-only dependency selecting the pinned cudart and
curand packages; it must remain for PyCOLMAP dependency consistency. Its wheel
contains no discovered separate licence file. Its treatment under NVIDIA's CUDA
terms must be resolved by the publisher before public distribution; an absence of
a licence file is not a grant of unrestricted rights.

## Python and scientific libraries

CPython and python-build-standalone retain their bundled licences, including those
of bundled libraries. Each installed wheel retains its own .dist-info licence
files and bundled third-party notices. Components include PyCOLMAP (BSD-3-Clause
and bundled dependencies), NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib and its fonts, Pillow,
contourpy, fonttools, kiwisolver, packaging, pyparsing, python-dateutil, cycler,
and six. The authoritative inventory is runtime-lock.json plus the actual
python-packages.txt and system-packages.txt generated by the build.

Ubuntu packages retain /usr/share/doc package copyright/licence material. Their
individual conditions (including source-availability obligations where applicable)
are not superseded by this document. Preserve all upstream notices.
